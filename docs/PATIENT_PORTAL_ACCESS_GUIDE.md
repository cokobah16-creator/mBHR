## 🔐 Registration Requirements

To register for the patient portal, you need:

### 1. Existing Patient Record

- You must have visited the facility before
- Your information must be in the system

### 2. Phone Number

- The phone number you enter **must match** your patient record
- Format: Can include country code (e.g., +234 XXX XXX XXXX)

### 3. Date of Birth

- Your DOB **must match** your patient record exactly
- Format: YYYY-MM-DD

### 4. Email (Optional)

- Email address for backup notifications
- Not required for registration

---

## 📋 Step-by-Step Registration

### Step 1: Visit Registration Page

Navigate to `/patient/register` or click "Create Account" from landing page

### Step 2: Enter Your Information

- **Phone Number:** Enter the phone number from your patient record
- **Email:** (Optional) Enter your email address
- **Date of Birth:** Enter your DOB matching patient records
- **Terms:** Check the box to accept terms and privacy policy

### Step 3: Request Verification Code

- Click "Continue to Verification"
- A 6-digit code will be sent to your phone via SMS

### Step 4: Enter Verification Code

- Enter the 6-digit code you received
- Code expires in 10 minutes
- You can request a new code if needed

### Step 5: Access Your Dashboard

- After successful verification, you're automatically logged in
- You'll be redirected to your dashboard

---

## 🔑 Step-by-Step Login

### Step 1: Visit Login Page

Navigate to `/patient/login` or click "Login to Portal" from landing page

### Step 2: Enter Phone Number

- Enter your registered phone number
- Click "Continue"

### Step 3: Request Verification Code

- A new 6-digit code will be sent to your phone
- Wait for SMS (may take 1-2 minutes)

### Step 4: Enter Verification Code

- Enter the 6-digit code
- Click "Verify & Login"

### Step 5: Access Your Dashboard

- You're logged in!
- You'll see your medical dashboard

## 🔍 Common Access Issues

### "No Patient Record Found"

**Cause:** Phone number or date of birth doesn't match records

**Solutions:**

1. Double-check phone number format
2. Verify DOB is correct (YYYY-MM-DD)
3. Try alternate phone numbers you've used
4. Contact the clinic to verify your records

### "Verification Code Not Received"

**Causes & Solutions:**

1. **SMS Delay:** Wait 2-3 minutes, codes can be delayed
2. **Wrong Number:** Verify you entered correct phone number
3. **Poor Signal:** Check your phone has cell signal
4. **Spam Filter:** Check your SMS spam folder
5. **Wait Time:** Can request new code after 10 minutes

### "Account is Locked"

**Cause:** Too many failed OTP attempts (5 failed attempts)

**Solution:**

- Wait 1 hour before trying again
- Contact support if urgent access needed

### "Session Expired"

**Cause:** Inactive for 30 minutes or session duration exceeded

**Solution:**

- Simply login again with your phone number
- New OTP code will be sent
