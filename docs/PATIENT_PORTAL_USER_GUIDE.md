## Getting Started

### What is the mBHR Patient Portal?

The mBHR Patient Portal is a secure online platform that gives you 24/7 access to your health information. Through the portal, you can:

- View your medical records and visit history
- Check your vital signs and lab results
- Request appointments with your healthcare provider
- Communicate with your care team
- Receive important health notifications

### Requirements

To use the patient portal, you need:

- A smartphone, tablet, or computer with internet access
- A phone number that matches our patient records
- Your date of birth

---

## Registration

### Step 1: Access the Registration Page

1. Visit the mBHR website
2. Click on "Patient Portal" in the menu
3. Click "Register here" link on the login page

### Step 2: Enter Your Information

You'll need to provide:

- **Phone Number:** This must match the phone number in your patient record
- **Email Address:** (Optional) For backup verification and notifications
- **Date of Birth:** This must match your date of birth in our records

### Step 3: Verify Your Identity

1. After entering your information, you'll receive a 6-digit verification code via SMS
2. Enter the code on the verification screen
3. The code expires in 10 minutes, so enter it promptly

### Step 4: Accept Terms

Read and accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy to complete registration.

### Important Notes

- Your phone number and date of birth must match our patient records exactly
- If you receive an error saying no matching patient record was found, please contact our office
- Keep your verification code private and never share it with anyone

## Login

### Step 1: Access the Login Page

1. Visit the mBHR website
2. Click on "Patient Portal"
3. Enter your phone number

### Step 2: Verify Your Identity

1. You'll receive a 6-digit verification code via SMS
2. Enter the code on the verification screen
3. Click "Verify & Login"

### Security Tips

- Each login requires a new verification code
- Codes expire after 10 minutes
- After 5 failed attempts, your account will be locked for 1 hour
- Never share your verification codes with anyone

## Medical History

### Viewing Your Visit History

1. From the dashboard, click "View Medical History" or select "Medical History" from the menu
2. You'll see a list of all your past visits
3. Each visit shows:
   - Date of visit
   - Chief complaint
   - Vital signs
   - Diagnosis
   - Provider name
   - Medications prescribed

### Viewing Visit Details

1. Click on any visit to see complete details
2. You'll see four sections:

**Vital Signs**
- Blood pressure, heart rate, temperature, oxygen levels
- Height, weight, and BMI

**Consultation Notes**
- Your symptoms (Subjective)
- Physical examination findings (Objective)
- Doctor's assessment
- Treatment plan

**Diagnosis**
- All diagnoses from that visit

**Medications**
- All prescriptions from that visit with dosage and directions

### Filtering and Searching

- Use the filter button to search by date range
- Results are sorted by most recent first
- Click "Load More" to see older visits

## Appointments

### Requesting an Appointment

1. From the dashboard, click "Request Appointment"
2. Fill out the request form:

**Appointment Type**
- General Consultation
- Follow-up Visit
- Chronic Disease Management
- Immunization
- Health Screening
- Prenatal Care
- Pediatric Care
- Mental Health
- Injury Assessment
- Other

**Preferred Dates**
- Choose up to 3 preferred dates
- Select time preferences (morning, afternoon, evening, or any time)

**Reason for Visit**
- Describe your symptoms or reason (required, minimum 10 characters)

**Additional Notes**
- Any extra information you'd like us to know (optional)

3. Click "Submit Request"

### What Happens Next?

- Your request is sent to our scheduling team
- They will review your request and available times
- You'll be contacted to confirm the appointment
- You'll receive a notification once your appointment is scheduled

### Viewing Your Appointments

- All scheduled and pending appointments appear on your dashboard
- Click on an appointment to see details
- You can cancel an appointment up to 24 hours before the scheduled time

## Messages

### Sending a Message

1. Click "Message Care Team" from the dashboard or messages icon
2. Write your message (include relevant details)
3. Click "Send"

### Reading Messages

1. Click on the Messages card or icon
2. Unread messages are marked with a badge
3. Click on a message to read it
4. You can reply to create a conversation thread

### What to Use Messages For

**Appropriate Uses:**
- Non-urgent questions about your health
- Prescription refill requests
- Follow-up questions after a visit
- Scheduling concerns

**Do NOT Use Messages For:**
- Medical emergencies (call emergency services)
- Urgent symptoms (call the clinic directly)
- Sensitive information like passwords

### Response Time

- Messages are typically answered within 1-2 business days
- For urgent matters, call the clinic directly

## Troubleshooting

### I Didn't Receive My Verification Code

**Possible Solutions:**
1. Wait a few minutes - SMS can be delayed
2. Check that you entered the correct phone number
3. Make sure you have cell signal or WiFi
4. Request a new code (available after 10 minutes)
5. Contact support if the problem persists

### I Entered the Wrong Code Too Many Times

- Your account is locked for 1 hour after 5 failed attempts
- Wait 60 minutes and try again
- Make sure you're using the most recent code
- Contact support if you need immediate access

### I Can't Find My Visit in the History

**Possible Reasons:**
1. The visit might be very recent and not yet closed
2. The visit may have been at a different facility
3. There may be a delay in updating records

Contact the clinic if you have questions about a specific visit.

### The Portal Isn't Loading or Is Very Slow

**Try These Steps:**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Refresh the page
3. Try a different browser
4. Clear your browser cache and cookies
5. Restart your device

### I Forgot Which Phone Number I Used

- Try all phone numbers you've used at our clinic
- Contact our office - we can look up your portal account

## Privacy & Security

### How We Protect Your Information

- **Secure Login:** Two-factor authentication with SMS codes
- **Encryption:** All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- **Access Logs:** We track who accesses your records and when
- **Session Timeout:** Automatic logout after 30 minutes of inactivity

### Your Responsibilities

- **Keep Codes Private:** Never share verification codes
- **Log Out:** Always log out when finished, especially on shared devices
- **Secure Device:** Use a password-protected device
- **Report Issues:** Contact us immediately if you suspect unauthorized access

### What We Track

For security and compliance, we log:
- Login attempts and times
- Which records you view
- Messages you send
- Your IP address and device information

### Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your medical records
- Request corrections to your information
- Know who has accessed your records
- Request your account be deleted
